## Concrete problem and behavior

Recent local battle logs showed several enemies receiving the same first move destination, four hunters still counted after the living enemy roster had fallen to one/zero, and repeated attack refreshes. The previous controller also chose only the player as its explicit attack target and left all friendly AI on map orders.

- Each surviving AI hull retains a fixed slot for this encounter. Twelve approach slots have at least 30 m separation; approach arrival is measured against that hull's assigned destination, rather than the center of the formation. This replaces clamped lateral spawn offsets that could converge on exactly the same point.
- Both teams select from their own current native `visibleTargets` only, choosing living opposing hulls. A 60 m preference for the current visible target prevents small distance changes from cancelling aim; a 45 m cost per other assigned attacker encourages sharing equally reachable opponents. A much closer threat can still take priority. Neither preference changes native weapon accuracy, penetration, detection or damage.
- Friendly AI without a visible opponent follows support slots behind and to the sides of the current living player hull, with at least 26 m between nominal slots and 40 m clearance from the player. When the player dies, surviving allies keep fighting around the last valid support anchor until native player control transfers to a living hull.
- Enemy pursuit shares the latest actual team sighting, which may be the player or an ally. Contact selection also retains a 60 m preference for the current visible contact. Hidden target positions are not used to update pursuit. The initial player spawn position remains the deliberate starting search clue. On reaching their approach positions, enemies search the existing deterministic local rings.
- A progressing native move is no longer cancelled just because ten seconds elapsed. Missing move tasks are retried after ten seconds. Active attack tasks receive a 45 s safety refresh; a missing attack token is retried after eight seconds. A changed target or engagement mode respects a 2.5 s minimum interval.
- Stop-to-fire hysteresis enters at 280 m and stays active until 340 m, avoiding repeated brake/accelerate toggles near one boundary. Friendly attack approach is native `Balanced`; enemy approach remains native `Aggressive`. A 1.5 s visibility grace preserves an existing firing task through detector flicker without issuing a new hidden-target attack order.
- Movement with less than 3 m progress over eight seconds can request a short alternating sidestep through the existing native pathfinder. The native driver gets up to twelve seconds for this detour before returning to the goal. Search legs get thirty seconds rather than eight. All new waypoint endpoints require terrain and a slope of at most 25 degrees. No vehicle teleportation or physics inputs are used.
- Living crew is checked at the 4 Hz roster scan, following the existing outcome observer's semantics: completely readable zero living crew removes a hull from AI control/target candidates; unknown data does not prove death. Per-frame Harmony guards use cached claims and immediately reject control of a player-controlled hull.

## Player orders, scope and fallback

Any external order to an automatically supported ally is accepted and permanently hands that hull back to native ordering for this battle. Its formation updates are also released. Switching player control into a supported hull releases that hull as well. There is no delayed automatic order that reclaims it. The next battle clears these temporary claims/yields.

Important limitation: the verified native `Order` object does not identify its sender. Therefore this conservative handoff also accepts a late map-script or other mod order as external. Such an ally may stop receiving automatic support; the log explicitly records `ally slot N yielded to external ...`, and the HUD counts native/manual hulls. This limitation is intentional until a player-specific ingress is proven. No order-origin guessing or input interception is introduced.

The existing `Campaign/EnemyHunt` key and default are retained; its description now states that it controls both enemy pursuit and allied support. False keeps both teams on native map AI. Claims apply only while Expedition owns this session. A controller exception releases all claims and disables this optional controller until the next encounter. Saves, blueprints, eras, budgets, spawns, audio and refit handling are unchanged.

The existing reference-type Harmony hooks remain `CommanderAI.Order(Order)` and `UpdateFormationTasks()`. No native value-type/byref hook has been added. Signatures, including native `AttackApproachType.Balanced`, were read from current installed interop; GameAssembly SHA256 was verified as `3E58082804D97CF32FFD400E7427E767EF9576D23DB71B2BE96FBB7F825FC17B`.
