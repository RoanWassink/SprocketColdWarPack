# Sprocket Smoke Launchers

Smoke Launchers adds a bank of three smoke grenade tubes that you place on a hull or turret. One key fires all three grenades, and they burst into smoke screens.

Version 0.2.5. It needs [Keybinds](../Keybinds) for its key.

## What it adds

- The "Smoke Launcher Bank" part, available from 1 January 1945. It has three grenade tubes, can be mirrored and is placed like other external equipment. Loaded, it weighs 18 kg and costs 120 (60 for material, 60 for assembly).
- One loaded salvo of three grenades per bank. All banks on the tank fire together, up to 8 banks per volley. There is no reload during combat.
- A fresh salvo in every bank at the start of each Play or combat session. A bank that is spent stays spent for the rest of that session, also for copies and reloaded saves of the vehicle.
- Grenades that fly a short arc and open into a cloud where they land or at the end of the flight (after 5 seconds at most). A cloud lasts 25 seconds.
- A launch sound and a separate sound when the grenades open, both generated by the mod. One volume setting controls both.
- The German smoke launcher part dated 1 January 1942. Only the bank fires.

## How to use it

1. Open the tank designer and find "Smoke Launcher Bank" among the external equipment.
<!-- SCREENSHOT: smoke-launcher-part-list.png (the Smoke Launcher Bank in the parts list) -->
2. Place it on the hull or turret. Mirror it if you want a bank on each side.
<!-- SCREENSHOT: smoke-launcher-on-tank.png (a tank with two smoke launcher banks) -->
3. Open the game's Settings screen, go to Controls, and open the mod keybinds panel. Under Smoke Launchers, set a key for "Fire smoke salvo". The mod sets no key itself.
4. Start a battle or a Play session and press your key. The grenades fly out and the smoke grows.
<!-- SCREENSHOT: smoke-screen.png (a smoke screen in front of a tank) -->

The smoke is visual only. It does not block the line of sight of computer-controlled units or of thermal sights. If you import a tank with a bank that is dated too early for the part, the bank stays on the tank, fires nothing and writes one warning to the log.

## Settings

The settings file is `BepInEx\config\sprocket.smokelaunchers.cfg`. The game creates it at the first start.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `General / Enabled` | `true` | Allows firing. Switching it off clears the smoke effects, and never refills the grenades. |
| `Audio / LaunchVolume` | `0.6` | Volume of the launch and opening sounds, from 0 to 1. At 0 both are muted. |

## Known limits

- The author's notes say that version 0.2.3 ran without crashes and reloaded the banks after a Play restart.
- For 0.2.5, these points are listed as not yet tested in the game: the stronger launch and opening sounds, the 1945 date limit (the bank can be picked on a Cold War vehicle), and the rules for reloading a bank per session.
- The pack was renamed recently and has not been tested in the game again since.
- The mod was written for game version 0.2.56.2.

## Building

The project is a .NET project in this folder (`SprocketSmokeLaunchers.csproj`, net6.0). Build it with `dotnet build SprocketSmokeLaunchers.csproj -c Release -p:GameDir=<path to Sprocket folder>`. The Keybinds DLL is taken from `$(GameDir)\BepInEx\plugins\SprocketKeybinds` or from `-p:KeybindsDll=<path>`. Tests run with `dotnet run --project tests -c Release` and `Verify.ps1`.

Part data goes to `Sprocket_Data\StreamingAssets\Parts` (`smokeLauncherBankPart.json` and `germanSmokeLauncherPart.json`), with `smokeLauncherBank.xml` in the English localization folder. The plugin folder is `BepInEx\plugins\SprocketSmokeLaunchers\`.
